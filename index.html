About The Shop

Barbering done with attention to detail

Hall Of Fades Barbershop was built around a simple idea — a good haircut is about precision, not shortcuts. Every visit is treated as a chance to get the line, the blend, and the finish exactly right, whether you’re after a sharp skin fade or a classic clean-up.

We keep the shop professional and comfortable, with barbers who take the time to talk through what you actually want before the clippers come out. It’s a straightforward approach: listen, cut with care, and send you out the door looking sharp.

Conveniently located on Gessner Rd, Hall Of Fades is a barber shop Houston locals can drop into for fades, haircuts, and beard grooming without the guesswork.
